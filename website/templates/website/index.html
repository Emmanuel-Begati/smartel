The Problem
Challenges in Modern Agriculture

Globally, pests and diseases account for an estimated 20-48% reduction in crop yields annually. - (UN FAO) Delayed detection of crop pest and diseases accounts for 47% of crop loss in every farming season across the world. 38% CO2 Emission comes from fertilizers, Chemicals & Leading to soil degradation (UNEP)

In Sub-Saharan Africa, certain pests alone cause annual cereal grain losses of about 4.1 million metric tonnes. - (UNEP) We are addressing challenges of delayed pest and disease identification that often lead to loss of yield, use of heavy fertilizers that contribute significantly to CO2 emissions and soil degradation.
