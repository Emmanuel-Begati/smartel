The Problem
Challenges in Modern Agriculture

Globally, pests and diseases account for an estimated 20-48% reduction in crop yields annually (UN FAO). Delayed detection of crop pest and diseases accounts for 47% of crop loss in every farming season across the world.

In Sub-Saharan Africa, specific pests alone cause annual cereal grain losses of about 4.1 million metric tonnes (UNEP). Furthermore, 38% of CO2 emissions stem from the excessive use of fertilizers and chemicals, leading to significant soil degradation (UNEP).

{% comment %}

Smallholder farmers struggle with identifying pest and disease infestations early, often resorting to reactive and heavy chemical applications that damage soil health and increase production costs. The lack of affordable, real-time monitoring solutions has left them vulnerable to erratic yields and financial instability.
